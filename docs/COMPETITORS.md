## Positioning

Backdesk is **the profitability layer on top of a PMS, not a PMS replacement.** A PMS answers "who is staying and at what price"; Backdesk answers "how much does this unit really make, and why", including the costs a PMS does not see (rent, setup, depreciation, utilities, maintenance). It ingests the numbers the host already has (a payout file, a receipt, a typed expense) and never operates the property. Its edge in Egypt is Arabic-first, phone-first, EGP-native, and cheaper than the host's time spent in a spreadsheet.

What this implies:
- Never add calendar, booking or channel features to close a gap with a PMS (permanent non-goal).
- The comparison that matters at launch is the spreadsheet, then HostPayout-style import tools.
- Anything claimed against a competitor must be checked first; this file is not that check.

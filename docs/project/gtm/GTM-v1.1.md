## Executive strategy

We market the free calculator in public and validate paying buyers in private. Paid media buys message learning (EGP 10,000 cap), not customers.

**What we sell now**

- **Public, free:** «حاسبة ربحية الشقة» at backdeskapp.com, no account needed. This is the only product claim we advertise.
- **Private, paid (gated):** a founder-prepared monthly P&L per apartment for co-hosts («تقرير مالي شهري مُعد يدويًا»). It is a service, not software. Pilot: 2 customers, ≤5 apartments.
- **Not yet:** Backdesk Pro software. Software pre-orders open only after gate G6.

**To whom**

- **Acquisition:** sublet (rental-arbitrage) operators in Greater Cairo and Alexandria, at the "before I sign the lease" moment.
- **Revenue hypothesis:** co-hosts and managers with 3–30 apartments.
- **Organic only:** owner-operators with 1–3 units and aspiring hosts.

**How**

1. Product truth first: every number and feature is verified before publication.
2. One content idea per week, cut into 3–4 assets from six reusable masters.
3. Facebook groups (value-first, with permission) and the Facebook page are the lead channels. WhatsApp is the hand-raise channel.
4. 15 structured interviews by November 8 decide vocabulary and segment.
5. Two paid creative tests (November 9 and 23, EGP 4,000 each) decide framing and format.
6. A concierge pilot in December measures willingness to pay and production time.

**What we will not do**

- Advertise unverified features, earnings outcomes or market benchmarks.
- Fabricate testimonials, screenshots, customer statements or a synthetic founder.
- Present feasibility as legal permission to sublet.
- Install a pixel or retarget, or claim user-level attribution.
- Count concierge sales as software pre-orders.
- Spend in the GCC before January 16.

**Expected end state on January 16:** verified calculator funnel data, a tested hook and format, 15+ interviews synthesised, 1–2 concierge customers with measured delivery time, and an evidence-based decision on paid acquisition and software pre-orders. The 20-pre-order gate will probably not be reached in this window; that is acceptable.

**G1 finding (Oct 18, 22:15 Cairo, production, read-only).** G1 fails as built: campaign attribution does not survive to the result. Events post to `/api/e` (no cookie). The shipped code reads only `?from=`, reduces it to `entry: "landing"` or `"other"`, then deletes it from the URL; `utm_source`/`utm_content` are ignored. A test visit with `?from=g1audit&utm_source=g1audit&utm_content=t1a` produced `verdict_shown {entry: "other", outcome: "loss", locale, display_mode}`: no source, no variant, no operating model. Events seen in the calculator: `first_run_started` (first visit only) and `verdict_shown` (once per page load). No `calc_start` per step and no `wa_cta_click` yet.

**Fix (Claude Code, no new storage or identifiers):** on first load, read `utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` (whitelisted, ≤32 chars, lowercase slug) into the same in-memory module state that already holds `entry`; attach `src`, `campaign`, `variant` and `model` to every event; add `calc_start` on the first answer; keep `entry`. Then re-run this test: G1 passes when a tagged visit shows the same `src` and `variant` on `first_run_started` and `verdict_shown`.
